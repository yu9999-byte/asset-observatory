The right licence for your product

Enterprise is the only plan that lets you redistribute or share CoinPaprika data. It needs a dedicated contract.

Keyless

Personal use. Side projects, learning and prototypes. Not for a commercial product.

Starter to Ultimate

Commercial use inside your company. Your own tools, models, dashboards and reports.

Enterprise

Redistribution. Show, share or resell the data in a product that your own customers use.

Building a customer-facing product? Paid commercial use and redistribution are different rights. Agree your display and distribution needs with the team before launch; Enterprise licensing covers negotiated distribution rights. The standard API terms require a “Powered by CoinPaprika” credit.

Describe the actual distribution path

Tell the team whether data appears in a customer-facing application, is exported in reports, is exposed through your API, or is resold. Your Enterprise agreement defines the permitted distribution, attribution and other negotiated terms.
